Method

Daily Event Order

The same order is applied to every target scenario.

  1. Update open positionsRead each position’s daily OHLC bar.
  2. Apply hard exitsUse the intraday −75% low and the five-year exit first.
  3. Apply profit targetUse the daily High for an intraday target touch.
  4. Recycle proceedsSale proceeds return to cash before new entries.
  5. Process new betsRecord a skipped bet when the original position cost exceeds cash.

Interpretation

Read the Portfolio Outcome, Not Just Hit Rate

Final portfolio valueMeasures wealth after each simulated sale proceeds are recycled into later bets.
CAGR and drawdownShow annualized efficiency and the cost of the worst peak-to-trough decline.
Capital lockedShows money still tied up in positions more than 20% underwater at the end.
Skipped betsShows opportunities the strategy could not take because cash was already committed.
