Model assumptions & coverage

−15% exits at 85% of entry; −75% exits at 25% of entry. Daily Low triggers the stop, with gap-down fills at the lower daily Open. Stops begin after purchase day because entry time is unknown. A touch on an actual sale date is assumed to occur before sale. No re-entry, reinvestment, additional profit target, fees or corporate-action adjustments. This is not an execution guarantee.

{{ summary.stop_loss.excluded }} bets are excluded from both simulation baselines for insufficient history or valuation. {{ a.missing_values }} actual valuations are unavailable. Actual-all totals remain shown separately.
